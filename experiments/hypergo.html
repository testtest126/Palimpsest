HyperGo — a 3D Go prototype

Real Go rules generalized to an N×N×N cubic lattice: groups and liberties over the 6-neighborhood (±x, ±y, ±z), capture, suicide illegal unless it captures, simple ko. The cube is shown as its N z-layers, laid out side by side — click an intersection on any layer to play there. Standalone prototype, not part of the published Palimpsest piece.

Core engine (neighbor/adjacency, group/liberty/capture, ko) is written parameterized by dimension count — every function takes dims and n explicitly, so 4D and 5D are a parameter change, not a rewrite. Exposed as window.HyperGoEngine for direct testing.
